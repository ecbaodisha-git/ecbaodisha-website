WHO WE ARE

Supporting the people behind poultry farming.

ECBA ODISHA stands for the All Odisha Environment Control Broilers Welfare Association. Our focus is the welfare, financial stability and fair treatment of farmers operating environment-controlled broiler sheds.

EC farms require substantial investment in housing, cooling, ventilation, electricity, labour and biosecurity. We work to ensure those realities are recognized in industry discussions.

  • Advocacy for transparent growing contracts
  • Recognition of EC infrastructure and operating costs
  • Constructive dialogue with integration companies
