Broiler Health Assistant

A bilingual English / ଓଡ଼ିଆ guide for recognizing common warning signs and taking safer first steps.

FARMER RESOURCE

Early attention helps protect your flock.

This educational tool offers general guidance only. It cannot diagnose diseases, choose vaccines or prescribe treatment. Consult a qualified poultry veterinarian for illness, mortality or vaccination decisions.

What to check first

  • Age of birds and number affected
  • Feed and water consumption
  • Ventilation, temperature and humidity
  • Mortality trend and recent changes
